Our Menu

Here at D&D Fair Foods, we believe that simple is better. That is why we have taken 3 fair favorites and crafted them to perfection with our own spin. Our most popular item is our hand-battered cheese curds, with gooey cheese in every bite. Our second specialty is an absolute fair classic, funnel cakes. This favorite comes with your choice of toppings and is guaranteed to make your day a lot sweeter. Last but certainly not least is the kid favorite deep fried Oreo's. These sweet treats will always have you going back for more with every bite. We are allergy conscious and fry all of our food in soybean oil as well. We also serve fresh cut french fries, corn dogs, cotton candy, and snow cones at a select few events.

Our cheese curds are fresh, never frozen, and are deep fried perfection. They start in Elsworth, Wisconsin where we pick them up fresh off the line at Elsworth Co-op Creamery. Then we batter them in house and deep fry them to that crispy golden perfection and make sure that cheese pull is perfect with every bite. You have a choice of ketchup or ranch to dip them in and enjoy! Allergies: Dairy, Gluten

Our funnel cakes start with a sweet bread batter that is similar to a donut. We then pour it out of a traditional metal funnel into the fryer and cook it until it is time to flip. After the flip one side is fried to golden brown and when the other side is finished cooking the topping begins. We have 13 different toppings available including classics such as powdered sugar and cinnamon sugar as well as a variety of fruits, frostings, and syrups. We also serve specialty funnel cakes, S'mores and Maple Bacon, at the Minnesota State Fair! Allergies: Dairy, Gluten, Eggs

Our deep fried oreos start with the classic oreo cookie, dipped into our funnel cake batter, and deep fried so that the cookie is soft inside. We then drizzle Hershey's chocolate syrup on top and dust the whole thing with powdered sugar. It is a sugary creation that is sure to satisfy any sweet tooth.
